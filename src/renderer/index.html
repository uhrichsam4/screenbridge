ScreenBridge

Share this screen, or watch another computer on your Wi-Fi.

Share this computer

Only share on a network you trust (home Wi-Fi). The stream isn't encrypted.

On the other computer, open ScreenBridge and type:

Address
Password

ScreenBridge needs permission to share your screen. In System Settings, turn on ScreenBridge under Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen ScreenBridge and press Start sharing again.

The other computer can watch, but can't use your mouse and keyboard yet. To allow that, turn on ScreenBridge under Accessibility, then stop and start sharing again.
